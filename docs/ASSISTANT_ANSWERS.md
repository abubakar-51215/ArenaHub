# ArenaHub assistant: what it answers and where each sentence comes from

The assistant (`POST /api/v1/assistant/ask`, signed-in players only) is rule based: no outside AI
service, nothing made up. It matches the question to one intent (`backend/app/modules/assistant/intents.py`:
keyword scoring over normalised words, Roman Urdu words mapped to English; a tie or a low score is
"not sure") and answers with the fixed sentences below (`backend/app/modules/assistant/service.py`),
filled in with the player's own data. It never promises a refund time, never shows a support email
or phone, and looks up a booking code only among the player's own bookings. Nothing is stored; the
log gets only the intent name (`assistant_intent`), never the words or who asked.

Checked 2026-10-14 against the code. Content rules: `docs/G6_CONTENT_CHECK.md`.

| Intent | Answer (with the player's data in brackets) | Source of each sentence |
|---|---|---|
| `next_booking` | "Your next game is [Tue 13 Oct at 7:00 PM], [arena], [court]. Booking code [AHB-...]." + Open booking. None: "You have no upcoming bookings." + Find an arena | The player's upcoming booking groups, soonest first (`booking/group_service.list_groups`, tab "upcoming"); the code is `booking_code_for`. |
| `bookings_count` | "You have [n] upcoming bookings, [n] past bookings and [n] cancelled bookings. You can see them all in Bookings." | The three tabs' totals of `list_groups` (the same split as the Bookings screen, `tab_of`). |
| `how_to_book` | "Search for a sport or an arena, open the arena, pick a court and a free time, then go on to pay. Your time is only booked once the arena confirms your payment." + Find an arena | The phone flow Search > arena > court times > booking > payment. A bank-transfer booking stays `pending_approval` until the arena approves (`payment/service.py`; G6 check, bank transfer row 5). |
| `bank_transfer` | The FAQ answer, word for word: "Pick Bank transfer when you pay. Send the exact amount to the arena account shown, with the reference shown, then upload a photo of your receipt (JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB). The arena checks it and confirms or turns down your booking." | `frontend/mobile/constants/support.ts` FAQ 3; each sentence checked in G6_CONTENT_CHECK, "How does bank transfer work?". |
| `awaiting_payment` | "A booking waits for payment until it is paid. With Bank transfer, send the exact amount to the arena account shown, then upload your receipt. A booking not paid within 24 hours is cancelled and its time is freed." + "[code] is waiting for payment." + Open booking. Receipt sent: "[code] is waiting for the arena to check your receipt. Your time is only booked once the arena confirms your receipt." | `booking/service.auto_cancel_stale_bookings` (`STALE_PENDING_PAYMENT_AFTER = 24 hours`, frees the slot); the bank screen and FAQ 3; statuses `pending_payment` / `pending_approval`. |
| `cancel` | "Open My bookings, tap the booking, then tap Cancel booking. You will see how much comes back before you confirm." + "For [code]: [the arena's own refund rule for that booking]" + Open booking | FAQ 1 (G6 check). The rule is the booking's `cancellation_note` = `describe_refund_policy(arena.refund_policy, start)`, the same words the booking screen shows. |
| `move` | "You can move a booking once, before its free cancellation time ends: open the booking and tap Reschedule." + "[code] can be moved until [7:00 AM on 13 Oct]." or "[code] can no longer be moved." + Open booking | `booking/group_service._plan_reschedule`: refused after `free_cancel_cutoff` ("It is too late to reschedule this booking.") and after one move ("A booking can be rescheduled only once."); `can_reschedule` of the booking details; the button is "Reschedule". |
| `refund_status` | Pending (or a failed try): "Your refund of [₨ 3,000] for [code] is waiting. The arena sends it back by bank transfer and you will get a message when it is done." Sent: "Your refund of [₨ 3,000] for [code] was sent to your bank." None: "You have no refunds. When you cancel, we show how much comes back before you confirm." + Open payment history | The player's latest refund (`payment/repository.list_payments_for_player`, `list_refunds_for_payment`). Bank-transfer refunds are sent by the arena and marked paid (`/owner/refunds/{id}/mark-paid`, "Refund completed" notice): G6 check, refund rows 3 and 4. No number of days anywhere (G6: "How long it takes depends on the arena"). |
| `matches_how` | "A match is a game you post from one of your confirmed bookings so other players can join. You settle the cost with each other. No payment happens in the app." + Open my matches | `match/service.py`: "Only a confirmed booking can have a match."; G6 check, match rows ("You settle it with each other. No payment happens in the app."). |
| `join_match` | "Open Play, then Open matches, tap a match and tap Join match. If it is full, someone else got the last place." + Open my matches | Play tab "Open matches"; the button "Join match"; join answers 409 "This match is full." (G6 check). |
| `rate_arena` | "You can rate an arena after your game there is over: open Bookings, Past, and tap Rate this game on that booking." | `review/service.py`: "Only completed bookings can be reviewed."; the past booking card's "Rate this game" (`components/booking/booking-group-card.tsx`). |
| `report_review` | "Open the arena's reviews, tap the three dots on the review and choose Report this review. The team looks at it." | `app/arena/[id]/reviews.tsx` (menu "Report this review"); reports go to the admin review queue (`review_admin_router`). |
| `change_email_password` | "Email: Profile, Personal information, then Change next to your email. Enter the new address and your password; we send a 6-digit code to the new address. Password: Profile, Settings, Change password; we email you a code, and every device is signed out after the change." | `user/service.request_email_change` (password checked, code to the new address); `request_password_change` / `verify_password_change` (`bump_session_epoch`); the Profile and Settings rows. |
| `delete_account` | "Profile, Settings, Delete account. It is refused while you have upcoming or unpaid bookings: cancel them or wait until they are done, then delete your account." | `user/service.delete_account` (the server's own refusal words); the Settings row "Delete account". |
| `what_is_futsal` | "Futsal is five-a-side football on a small court. In ArenaHub, a search for football or 5-a-side shows futsal arenas." + Find a futsal arena | The general meaning of the word; the search rule is the smart-search alias list (`ai/parser.py`: football, soccer, 5-a-side -> futsal). |
| `near_me` | "Near me sorts arenas by distance from you. It needs your location: if location is off, the app says so and shows arenas in your city instead." + Open search | Search sort "near" (`arena/repository.search_public_arenas`); the Results banner "Location is off. Showing arenas in ...". |
| `notifications` | "Profile, Notifications shows your messages. In Profile, Settings you can turn booking updates, reminders, payments and refunds, and matches on or off." | Profile row "Notifications"; the four switches of `app/profile/settings.tsx`. |
| `app_problem` | "If the app says you are offline, connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data; we load things again when you are back online. If something still does not work, write to support." + New complaint | `lib/network.ts` (`OFFLINE_TEXT`); support = the in-app complaint (no email or phone is shown). |
| `not_sure` | "I am not sure about that one. You can ask me about bookings, refunds, payments or matches, or write to support." + New complaint, chips "When is my next booking?" and "Where is my refund?" | Fixed fallback (frame 9 of arena-hub-player-app-smart.html). |

Question chips (`SUGGESTIONS`): each intent has one, sent as `suggestion` instead of words. The
greeting chips on the phone are "When is my next booking?", "How do I cancel?", "Where is my
refund?", "How do I pay by bank transfer?" (frame 6).

Re-read this table with the FAQ when card or wallet payments go live (refunds would then be sent by
the gateway, and the refund and bank-transfer sentences change).
