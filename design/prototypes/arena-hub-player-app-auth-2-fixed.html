Motion rules used in these frames (for every mobile screen)

  • Press: buttons dip to 97.5% in 160 ms. Hover (web preview only): lime fill sweeps up.
  • Loading: label fades, a spinner appears and the button keeps its width. Success: a short tick. Error: one gentle shake.
  • Pop-ups: dim + blur fades in, sheet rises with an ease-out in about 340 ms. Closing is faster (about 200 ms). Destructive actions use the red button and always have a plain “Stay / Cancel”.
  • Toasts: drop from the top, stay about 3 seconds, slide away.
  • Fields: border turns ink with a soft lime ring on focus. The mail and lock icons are huge while the field is empty and idle (the field grows tall), and shrink to normal size with a smooth 300 ms animation when the field is focused or has text.
  • Everything respects “reduce motion”: animations become instant.
